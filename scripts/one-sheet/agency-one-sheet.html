About Brett

Brett Lechtenberg is a thought leader in the fields of business and personal empowerment. His flow and peak performance-based transformational skills include entrepreneurial coaching, systems development and implementation, as well as personal mastery and family safety.

Brett holds an 8th-degree black belt in Tae Kwon Do and black belts in various other martial arts. Brett has used his martial arts school, Personal Mastery Martial Arts, as a laboratory for his personal development training, business systems, and his philosophies on personal mastery and flow state management for over 28 years.
